Solving SRE & DevOps complexity for cloud native teams

01_friction Your platform slows down teams
Most platform problems are not Kubernetes problems. They are ownership, delivery, reliability, and abstraction problems. I design internal developer platforms that reduce repeated setup, unclear boundaries, and painful release paths.
02_manual_ops Your infrastructure is too manual
Teams spend too much time managing clouds through consoles, manual scripts, or rigid Terraform setups. I build Crossplane based workflows that make infrastructure repeatable, reviewable, and safer under GitOps control.
03_fragile_runtime Kubernetes works, until it doesn't
When scale hits or incidents occur, teams lack operational clarity. I help teams strengthen cluster reliability, configure production-grade observability stacks, establish service level objectives, and prepare runbooks.
